Projections are this tracker's own model output, not media calls. Official results come from each state after certification.

Model: expected vote per county from 2022 and 2024 baselines with a turnout factor learned from completed counties; remaining vote split from each county's counted vote blended toward its baseline; 1000 Monte Carlo draws per race with shared national and statewide shifts, county turnout and split noise. A race is projected only when the leader wins at least 99.5% of draws, the trailing candidate would need 75% or more of 1.5 times the remaining vote, and at least 25% of the expected vote is in (50% in late-counting states). Projections can be withdrawn and stay marked as such. Times are Eastern.
